Title: C01 ED boarding
Points: 2
1.  A health system invests heavily in expanding emergency department (ED) capacity in a region where ED visits per capita have been climbing for a decade. Two years later, ED wait times are essentially unchanged, and ED visits per capita have continued to rise. Which explanation is most consistent with a systems-level account of this pattern?
a)  ED demand is fixed in the short run, so capacity expansions cannot affect wait times.
*b) Capacity expansion has reduced upstream barriers to ED use without altering the conditions producing demand, leaving the underlying drivers of ED utilization intact.
c)  Wait times are determined entirely by hospital staffing and are independent of patient volume.
d)  ED wait times always rise with capacity expansion because of regulatory delays in licensing new beds.
