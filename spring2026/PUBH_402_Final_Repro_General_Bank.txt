Title: RG09 Trigger ban dynamics
Points: 2
1.  Following the Dobbs decision, several states implemented "trigger ban" laws that prohibited most abortions. Researchers documented that the closure of clinics in those states was associated with an increase in clinic volumes in neighboring states without bans. From a systems perspective, which of the following best characterize this pattern? Select all that apply.
[*] Demand was not eliminated; it was redistributed across state lines, creating new geographic and financial barriers.
[*] The structural change shifted travel burden from in-state to out-of-state, concentrating costs on patients with fewer resources.
[ ] Closure of clinics in trigger-ban states reduced national demand for abortion services to near zero.
[*] The system response illustrates that state-level bans can produce regional rather than purely state-level effects on access.
[ ] The pattern shows that abortion bans had no measurable effect on access.
